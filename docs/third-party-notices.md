## Google assets

- `public/google-g.png` comes from [Google's identity assets](https://developers.google.com/identity/images/g-logo.png) and is used for the Google sign-in action under its [branding guidance](https://developers.google.com/identity/branding-guidelines).
- `public/fonts/google-sans-medium.woff2` is the Google Sans v71 Latin 500-weight webfont supplied by [Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Google+Sans). The [SIL Open Font License](../public/fonts/OFL.txt) is included alongside it. The font is served locally only for the Google button.
